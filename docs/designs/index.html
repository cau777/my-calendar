my-calendar: design pass

Mobile-first, dark only. Near-black surfaces, one periwinkle accent, and colour reserved for meaning: each course gets its own hue, parsed automatically from the D2L LOCATION field. Deadlines are shown as dashed checkable rows instead of time blocks. Week starts Sunday; times follow the phone's time zone. Data shown uses your real feed for Nov 2026; personal events are made up.

Accent CPSC 2190 ENGL 1123 MATH 1171 Personal Health Surface

Type

Inter, 4 weights. Titles use 24/700 with tight tracking, body 14/500, meta 12/400 in the muted grey. Times use tabular numerals so columns line up.

Surfaces

Three steps: #0a0b0e page, #121419 cards, #191c23 controls. Use 1px #262a33 hairlines instead of shadows; the only shadow is under the FAB.

Colour

The accent marks today, the primary action and the selected state, and nothing else. Course hues appear as a 3px bar, a 15% tinted chip, or a dot, never as a solid fill.

Motion

Sheets spring up in 220 ms. Views cross-fade and slide 12 px on swipe. A checkbox tick gives a light haptic (navigator.vibrate on Android). Respects reduced-motion.
